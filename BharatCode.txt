Project Name: KAAMSETU

Project Description:
KAAMSETU is an employment and entrepreneurship platform that helps people discover job opportunities, showcase their skills, connect with customers, and build digital businesses.

GitHub Repository:
https://github.com/Pocket34/KAAMSETU

Core Features:
- Employment discovery
- Company and application information
- Skill showcasing
- Digital business profiles
- Customer connections
- AI-powered assistance
- Hindsight persistent memory

Technology:
Next.js, TypeScript, FastAPI, Supabase, Hindsight AI Memory.
